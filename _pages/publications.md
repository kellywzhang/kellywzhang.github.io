<h2>Active Collaborations</h2>

<h4>Mobile Health Coaching to Increase Physical Activity</h4>
<p>My group is collaborating with medical researchers at Imperial and Stanford University on <a href="https://myheartcounts.stanford.edu/">My Heart Counts</a>, a global cardiovascular research study app with over 50,000 users. Our focus is developing a LLM-based digital health coach to deliver personalized intervention messages aimed at increasing physical activity. The intervention will use reinforcement learning to adapt and personalize messaging over time. Further work involves developing novel machine learning methods for the large-scale, passively collected wearable sensor data from the app.</p>
